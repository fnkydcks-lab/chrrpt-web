Use

Chrrpt is a voice messaging app. Use it to send voice messages to people you know. You must be 13 or older to use Chrrpt.

You own your voice messages. We don't listen to them. They delete automatically after 9 days. Don't send anything illegal or that you wouldn't want a reasonable person to hear.

You can delete your account anytime in Settings. When you do, your messages and profile data are permanently deleted.
