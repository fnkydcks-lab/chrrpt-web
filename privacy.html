Privacy Policy

Last updated May 2025
For Humans

I don't know who you are. I don't want to. One of the fields in my database has your email in it. I've never looked at it. Your voice messages delete themselves in 9 days because storing your stuff costs money and frankly isn't my business. I don't sell your data because I don't have your data. That's the whole policy. Also, Chrrpt is for people 13 and older. If I find out someone under 13 signed up, I'll delete their account immediately.

For Apple

Data Collection

Chrrpt collects a user-generated username, nickname, and color preference. Authentication via Apple Sign-In or Google Sign-In provides an email address. Chrrpt is intended for users 13 years of age and older. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13. If we become aware that a child under 13 has provided personal information, we will delete it immediately.

Data Retention

Voice messages are permanently deleted after 9 days.

Data Sharing

We do not sell or share user data.

Account Deletion

Delete your account anytime in Settings. To delete your account: open the app → tap the horn twice to open Settings → tap DELETE ACCOUNT → confirm. Everything goes with it immediately: your profile, nickname, all voice messages, audio files, and friendships. No data is retained after deletion.
